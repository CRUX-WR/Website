Client profile

jeeva.ai — AI sales platform, B2B + PLG (B2C)

The constraint we diagnosed

We ran a full PostHog funnel and cohort analysis across the PLG signup and activation flow, tracing every step from landing page to first value moment. The breakage clustered right at conversion: friction in the magic-link auth step and hesitation at the pricing exposure point, both compounding into self-serve users dropping off before they ever activated.

What we shipped

A/B/C tested magic-link onboarding and pricing tiers, repositioned jeeva.ai's end-to-end agentic sales search as the product's hero feature, and helped launch a new autonomous-agent product for onboarding and Salesforce workflows. Ran a 6-month, $6M paid growth program (2× ROAS) spanning both the B2B (sales-assisted) and PLG (self-serve) motions, across Meta, Google, LinkedIn, AdRoll, TikTok, and Reddit: daily budget scaled past $15,000, the funnel reached 1,000+ qualified leads a day (work email required) and 10,000+ landing page views a day, and LP-to-signup conversion hit 12% against a 2.5-5% industry benchmark, driven by creative testing and deep analytics rather than media spend alone.

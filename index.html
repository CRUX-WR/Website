Who we serve

Three profiles, one requirement: real data.

Pre-launch and early-stage startups are out of scope. Our diagnostic phase needs something to bite into: real usage, real cohorts, real support volume.

B2B

SaaS, Series A+

Has traction and needs to scale it. A buying committee, not a single end user, messaging built around positioning, sales enablement, and ABM.

B2C

Apps & platforms

Real usage data, not pre-launch. Funnel and cohort analysis only works with real traffic volume. This is the floor, not a nice-to-have.

D2C

Mid-market, $1M–$20M revenue

Has a real ops/data stack, not a founder running ads solo. Enough scale that lifecycle and retention fixes actually move revenue.

Why this matters: small D2C accounts rarely have enough traffic for statistically meaningful funnel analysis. Mid-market and B2B accounts do: real analytics, real cohorts, real support-ticket volume. That makes the diagnostic phase defensible, and the fixes that follow it worth shipping.
